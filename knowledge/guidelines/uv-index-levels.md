# UV index levels

The UV index measures ultraviolet radiation from the sun. The higher it is, the more it can harm
skin and eyes, and the faster harm happens.

The number reported for a day is usually its maximum. That comes in the four hours around solar
noon, which falls between about noon and 2 p.m.

## What to do at each level

- **0-2:** you can safely enjoy being outside.
- **3-7:** seek shade during the midday hours. Wear a shirt, sunscreen and a hat.
- **8 and above:** avoid being outside during the midday hours. Shade is essential, and a shirt,
  sunscreen and hat are a must.

## How to protect yourself

From 3 upwards there is an increased risk of skin damage, and protection is recommended. Good
protection combines several things:

- clothing
- a hat
- sunglasses
- shade
- sunscreen
