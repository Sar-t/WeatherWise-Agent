# NAQI Poor (201-300)

## Health effects

Most people may feel breathing discomfort if they are exposed for a long time.

## What to do

- Keep outdoor activity moderate, especially for at-risk groups.
- Sensitive people should reduce long periods of exertion outdoors and watch for symptoms.
- CPCB's general advice for high AQI periods is to limit outdoor activity, especially strenuous
  exercise, and to check daily AQI reports and health advisories before planning school, travel
  or outdoor work.
- Protect children, older people, and people with respiratory or heart disease.
