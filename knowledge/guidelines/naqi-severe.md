# NAQI Severe (401-500)

## Health effects

Air at this level affects even healthy people. It can seriously harm people who already have
respiratory or cardiovascular disease.

## What to do

- Avoid unnecessary outdoor activity.
- Consider wearing a mask.
- Reduce your exposure straight away.
- Protect children, older people, and people with lung or heart conditions.
- Check the daily AQI and local health advisories before any travel, school run or outdoor work.
