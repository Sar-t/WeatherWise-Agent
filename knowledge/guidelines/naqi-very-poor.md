# NAQI Very Poor (301-400)

## Health effects

The risk of respiratory illness rises with long exposure. Children, older people and people who
are ill need extra protection.

## What to do

- Limit time outdoors.
- Protect vulnerable groups.
- Follow local advisories.
- CPCB also suggests masks when pollution is severe, or when you can't avoid travelling during
  high pollution.
- Limit outdoor activity during high AQI periods, especially strenuous exercise.
