# NAQI Moderately Polluted (101-200)

This band is also called "Moderate". CPCB's public information page uses that name, while the
2014 NAQI table calls the same 101-200 range "Moderately Polluted".

## Health effects

People with lung disease or asthma, children and older adults may feel breathing discomfort.

## What to do

- Sensitive groups should cut down on long periods of exertion outdoors.
- CPCB's advice for the Moderately Polluted (Moderate) to Poor range is that sensitive people
  reduce prolonged outdoor exertion and watch for symptoms.
