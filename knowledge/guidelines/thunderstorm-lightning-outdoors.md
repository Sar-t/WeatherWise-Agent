# Thunderstorms and lightning outdoors

If you can hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck by lightning. Watch for darkening
skies and rising wind, and follow local warnings.

## If you are outdoors

- Get to a safe shelter straight away. Avoid metal structures and buildings with metal sheeting.
- Choose a low-lying spot if you can, but not one likely to flood.
- If you are caught in the open, crouch down with your feet together and your head down. Don't
  lie flat on the ground.
- Keep away from trees, hilltops, metal fences, and power or phone lines. Never shelter under a
  tree.
- Hair standing up on the back of your neck can mean lightning is about to strike.
- Rubber-soled shoes and car tyres do not protect you from lightning.

## If you are travelling

- Get off bicycles, motorcycles and farm vehicles, and get to a safe shelter.
- If you are boating or swimming, get to land as fast as you can.
- In a car, stay inside until the storm passes, with the windows up. Park away from trees and
  power lines, and don't touch metal inside. The metal roof gives protection.
