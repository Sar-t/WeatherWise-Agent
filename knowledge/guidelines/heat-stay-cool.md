# Staying cool in hot weather

Heat stress is a leading cause of weather-related deaths. It can also worsen existing illness.
Its health effects are largely preventable.

## Stay out of the heat

- Avoid going outside, and avoid strenuous activity, during the hottest part of the day.
- Stay in the shade. In the sun, it can feel 10-15°C hotter.
- Spend 2-3 hours of the day somewhere cool.
- Follow official heat warnings.

## Keep your body cool and hydrated

- Wear light, loose-fitting clothes.
- Take cool showers, or wet your skin with a damp cloth or spray.
- Drink water regularly: about a cup an hour, and at least 2-3 litres a day.

## Keep your home cool

- During the day, close windows and block direct sunlight. Open windows at night when it is
  cooler outside.
- Use electric fans only below 40°C. Above 40°C, a fan heats the body.

## Look out for others

Check regularly on vulnerable people, especially:

- people over 65
- people with heart, lung or kidney conditions
- people with a disability
- people living alone
