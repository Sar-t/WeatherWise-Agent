# Thunderstorms at home or work, and first aid

## Before the storm

- Stay indoors and avoid travel if you can.
- Close doors and windows.
- Secure loose objects outside, such as furniture and bins, and clear debris that could be blown
  about.
- Bring children and animals inside.
- Unplug appliances you don't need, to guard against power surges.

## During the storm

- Don't bathe or shower, and stay away from running water. Lightning can travel along metal pipes.
- Keep away from doors, windows, stoves, bathtubs and other conductors.
- Don't use corded phones or other wired electrical equipment.

## If someone is struck by lightning

- Take them to a hospital. Give basic first aid if you can.
- They carry no electrical charge and are safe to touch.
- Check for broken bones, loss of hearing or sight, and burns. Look where the current entered and
  where it left the body.
