# NAQI Good and Satisfactory (0-100)

India's National Air Quality Index (NAQI) turns several pollutant readings into one number. Each
pollutant gets a sub-index, and the highest one becomes the reported AQI and its category.

## Good (0-50)

Air quality poses little or no risk, and the health impact is minimal.

## Satisfactory (51-100)

The air is generally acceptable for most people. People who are sensitive may feel minor
breathing discomfort.

## What to do

At these levels, CPCB's public advice is that normal activity can continue, with routine
awareness. Checking the daily AQI before outdoor exercise, the school commute or other outdoor
plans is still a good habit, especially for vulnerable family members.
