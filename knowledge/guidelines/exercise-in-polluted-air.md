# Exercise on high-pollution days

This is the Health Ministry's advice for days when air quality is Poor or worse, meaning an AQI
above 200.

## Exercise and timing

- Avoid outdoor walks in the early morning and late evening.
- Avoid jogging, running and other strenuous physical activity outdoors.
- Change the timing of outdoor plans to fit the AQI. On Poor to Severe days, stay indoors where
  you can.
- Keep doors and windows shut in the early morning and late evening. If a room needs airing, do
  it between about 12 p.m. and 4 p.m.

## Reduce exposure

- Check your area's AQI before planning the day.
- Stay away from roads with slow, heavy traffic, construction or demolition sites, and areas near
  polluting industries.

## People at higher risk

Pregnant women, young children, older people, and people with chronic lung or heart disease
should limit time outdoors as much as they can. People with chronic lung or heart disease should
avoid any strenuous activity on these days.

## Symptoms to act on

See the nearest doctor promptly if you get breathlessness, cough, chest discomfort or pain,
giddiness, or red or watery eyes.
